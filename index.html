Privacy Policy for PhotoFind

Last updated: November 2026
🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Guarantee: PhotoFind is designed with privacy-by-design principles. All photo indexing, machine learning inference, and vector searches execute locally on your device. Your photos, search terms, and gallery metadata are never uploaded to any cloud server or third party.

1. Overview

PhotoFind ("we", "our", or "the App") provides an intelligent photo search tool for Android devices. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device

The App accesses your media files strictly on-device to provide its core functionality:

  • Photos and Gallery Media: The App reads images from your device gallery to extract visual features using an on-device neural network (MobileCLIP via ONNX Runtime).
  • Vector Embeddings: Extracted mathematical representations (vectors) are stored strictly inside your phone's private local SQLite database.
  • Search Queries: When you search for terms (such as "dog" or "receipt"), the text is processed locally on your phone's processor. Searches function completely offline, including in Airplane Mode.

3. Device Permissions

To function as an on-device gallery search engine, PhotoFind requests the following permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required solely to index and display photos located on your device. Photos are never transmitted outside your device.
  • INTERNET: Used exclusively to load third-party advertisements (Google AdMob) that keep PhotoFind completely free to use.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

PhotoFind is supported by advertising through Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and device hardware information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve ads.

For more information on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since PhotoFind does not operate cloud storage or user accounts, we do not store any of your personal data on remote servers. All locally indexed vectors and settings can be erased at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset Indexing" button in the App's settings, or
  • Clearing the App's storage via Android Settings > Apps > PhotoFind > Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the App from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

PhotoFind does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The App is a general-utility gallery tool suitable for all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository at: https://github.com/ChristianRM/photofind.
